<ContentSection title="What is W3OS?" description="A practical standard and community for operational security in Web3.">
  <p>
    W3OS is an open framework that gives teams a common language and set of expectations for operational security. It covers the risks that sit outside code: who has access, how keys and credentials are handled, how infrastructure and domains are protected, and how your organization responds when something goes wrong.
  </p>
  <p>
    The standard is designed to be used—by protocol teams, DAOs, foundations, and infra providers—to benchmark current posture, prioritize gaps, and communicate maturity to stakeholders and auditors.
  </p>
</ContentSection>

<ContentSection title="Why this matters" description="Many serious failures in Web3 stem from operational gaps, not smart contract bugs.">
  <p>
    High-profile incidents often involve compromised keys, social engineering, misconfigured access, or weak vendor and process controls. Smart contract audits are essential but do not address these areas. Operational security is what connects strong engineering to real-world resilience.
  </p>
  <p>
    W3OS helps teams address identity and access, domain and infrastructure security, repository and SDLC practices, communications and social surfaces, vendor risk, incident readiness, and governance—so that the whole system is aligned with the same expectations.
  </p>
</ContentSection>
