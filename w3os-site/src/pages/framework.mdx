<ContentSection title="Using the framework" description="The W3OS framework is a structured set of domains and controls. Use it to assess where you are, decide what to improve first, and communicate maturity.">
  <p>
    Each pillar of the framework covers a distinct area of operational security. Within each, controls are organized so you can see what a baseline looks like, what “strengthened” adds, and what “mature” looks like. You do not have to implement everything at once; the goal is to make progress in a clear order and to speak a common language with auditors and partners.
  </p>
  <p>
    Deeper implementation guidance, examples, and resources will expand over time with community input. The framework page on the canonical W3OS repository remains the source of truth for the full specification.
  </p>
</ContentSection>
